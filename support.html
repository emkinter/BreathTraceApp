Support

Answers to common questions about BreathTrace, and how to get in touch if yours isn’t here.

Using the guide

How do I choose a pattern or change its timing?

Tap the pattern’s name at the top of the screen and pick another one. Long exhale and Power open a submenu with their timings. On Apple Watch, tap the name to open the list of patterns.

How do I start, pause or restart a session?

The guide starts on its own. Use the play/pause button to stop and continue, and the reset button to begin the pattern again from the first breath.

What do the shapes mean?

The marker moves along one side of the shape for each phase of the breath, with one tick mark per second. Patterns that only breathe in and out use an oval, patterns with one hold use a triangle, and patterns with two holds use a square.

How long should I practice?

That is up to you. BreathTrace keeps repeating the pattern until you pause it. If you feel dizzy, lightheaded or unwell, stop and breathe normally.

Sound

How do I turn the sounds off?

Tap the speaker button. It mutes everything: the breath sounds, the gong at each change of phase and the tick on each second. Tap it again to turn them back on.

I can’t hear anything.
  • Check that the speaker button in BreathTrace isn’t muted.
  • Turn up the volume on your device, and check where the sound is playing if headphones or a speaker are connected.
  • The breath sounds are quiet by design. Headphones make them easier to hear.
Does it keep playing when my iPhone is locked?

Yes. On iPhone the sounds keep guiding you with the screen locked, and you can play and pause from the Lock Screen.

Haptics and Apple Watch

How do the haptic taps work?

On iPhone and Apple Watch, taps quicken and strengthen as you breathe in, then slow and soften as you breathe out. During a hold there are no taps. The hand button turns them on or off; it only appears on devices that have haptics.

I don’t feel any taps on my Apple Watch.
  • Check that the hand button in BreathTrace is turned on.
  • On the watch, open Settings > Sounds & Haptics and make sure Haptic Alerts is on.
  • Wear the watch snugly, unlocked, on your wrist.
  • The taps during a breath are gentle. The firmer tap at the start of each breath in and out is the easiest to notice.
My Apple Watch makes a sound at the start of each breath.

The firm tap at the start of a breath comes with a short system tone. Turn on Silent Mode on the watch to feel the tap without the tone.

Does a session continue when I lower my wrist?

Yes. On Apple Watch a session keeps going with your wrist down for up to an hour.

Apple Intelligence

What does the Apple Intelligence button do?

It opens a conversation about breathing. BreathTrace enters a question about the pattern you are using, for example “What is the Box breathing pattern?”, and you can go on to ask your own questions. The conversation lasts until you close the app or start a new one.

I don’t see the Apple Intelligence button.

The button only appears on an iPhone, iPad or Mac that supports Apple Intelligence. It is not available on Apple Watch. If the conversation says Apple Intelligence is off or still getting ready, turn it on in Settings > Apple Intelligence & Siri and give it time to finish downloading.

An answer was wrong or unhelpful.

Answers are written by the language model Apple Intelligence runs on your device. They can be wrong or incomplete, and they are not medical advice. Start a new conversation with the pencil button to try again.

Health and safety

Is BreathTrace safe for me?

BreathTrace is a breathing timer, not a medical device. Breath holds and slow or deep breathing aren’t right for everyone. Talk to your doctor before you start if you are pregnant, or have a heart, lung or blood pressure condition, epilepsy, or any other health concern. Read the full medical disclaimer, which is also available in the app at the end of the pattern list.

Privacy and data

What data does BreathTrace collect?

None. See the privacy policy.

How do I reset the app?

Delete BreathTrace and install it again. That removes your saved pattern and settings.

Requirements

BreathTrace needs iOS 26, iPadOS 26, watchOS 26 or macOS 26, or later. The Apple Watch app works on its own, without the iPhone nearby.

Contact

Found a problem, or have a question or a suggestion? Open an issue on GitHub and I’ll get back to you there. It helps to mention your device, its software version, and what you did before the problem appeared.

Issues on GitHub are public, so please don’t include personal or health information.
