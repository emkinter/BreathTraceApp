Medical Disclaimer

This is the disclaimer shown in the app before your first session.

BreathTrace is a breathing timer. It is not a medical device, and it doesn’t diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Nothing in it is medical advice or a substitute for care from a qualified health professional.

Breath holds and slow or deep breathing aren’t right for everyone. Talk to your doctor before you start if you are pregnant, or have a heart, lung or blood pressure condition, epilepsy, or any other health concern.

Stop and breathe normally if you feel dizzy, lightheaded, short of breath or unwell. Don’t practice in water, or anywhere that feeling dizzy or faint could put you or others in danger.

Answers from Apple Intelligence

On devices with Apple Intelligence, BreathTrace can answer questions about breathing patterns. Those answers are generated on your device by a language model. They can be wrong, and they are not medical advice.

Where to find this in the app

Tap the pattern’s name and choose Medical Disclaimer at the end of the list.
