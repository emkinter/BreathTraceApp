Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

In short: BreathTrace does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it makes no network requests.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the BreathTrace app for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, Apple TV and Apple Vision Pro (“the app”), published by Ekkehard Koch (“I”, “me”), and to this website.

Data the app collects

None. The app does not collect any information about you, and it does not send anything to me or to anyone else. It contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software.

Data that stays on your device

To remember how you like to practice, the app saves a few settings on your device:

  • the breathing pattern and timing you last chose,
  • whether sounds and haptic taps are turned on,
  • that you have read the medical disclaimer.

These settings are not linked to your identity and never leave the device through the app. Deleting the app deletes them. If you back up your device, for example with iCloud Backup, they may be included in that backup under Apple’s terms.

Apple Intelligence

On devices with Apple Intelligence turned on, the app offers a conversation about breathing patterns. Your questions are answered by the language model that Apple Intelligence runs on your device. The app does not send your questions or the answers to me or to any server, and it does not save the conversation: it is gone when you close the app or start a new conversation.

Health data

The app does not read from or write to the Health app, and it does not use any sensors to measure your breathing, heart rate or anything else about your body. On Apple Watch it runs a mindfulness session only so that the guide can keep going while your wrist is down.

Information from Apple

You download the app from Apple’s App Store, and Apple handles that under its own privacy policy. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device’s settings, Apple may show me aggregated, anonymous statistics, such as the number of downloads or crash reports. I cannot identify you from them.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you visit, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you contact me

If you ask for support, I receive whatever you choose to send, such as your GitHub user name and the text of your message. I use it only to answer you. Support requests made through GitHub Issues are public.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. If a future version of the app begins to collect any data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue on GitHub or see the support page.
