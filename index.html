Guided breathing patterns

Follow the shape. Breathe.

A marker traces an oval, a triangle or a square while a pair of lungs fills and empties in time, so you always know whether to breathe in, hold or breathe out, and for how long.

Coming soon to the App Store

For iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch and Mac. No account, no ads, no data collected.

Five patterns

From winding down to waking up

Relaxing

4–7–8

Breathe in, hold, then take a long, slow breath out. A pattern for winding down.

Triangle

4–4–4

An even inhale, hold and exhale, traced along three equal sides.

Box

4–4–4–4

Four equal sides, including a hold with empty lungs. Also known as square breathing.

Long exhale

4–6 · 4–8 · 3–1–7–1

Breathe out for longer than you breathe in, with no holds or only short pauses.

Power

1 : 4 : 2

Inhale for one count, hold for four, exhale for two. Choose a count of 3, 4, 5 or 8 seconds.

See it, hear it, feel it

Guidance you can follow with your eyes closed

  • A shape for every pattern. An oval for breathing in and out, a triangle for one hold, a square for two, with a countdown and a plain instruction for every phase.
  • Breath sounds that fit. A breath in and a breath out that last exactly as long as each phase, a soft gong at every change and a quiet tick on every second. One button mutes them all.
  • Haptics on iPhone and Apple Watch. Taps quicken and strengthen as you inhale, then slow and soften as you exhale.
  • Keeps going. On iPhone the sounds continue with the screen locked. On Apple Watch a session carries on with your wrist down.
  • Accessible. Works with VoiceOver, larger text sizes and Reduce Motion.

Apple Intelligence

Ask about a pattern

On devices with Apple Intelligence turned on, one button opens a conversation about the pattern you are using. BreathTrace asks what the pattern is, and you can go on with your own questions. Answers are written on your device and can be wrong; they are not medical advice.

Privacy

Nothing leaves your device

BreathTrace needs no account, makes no network requests and collects no data. Your pattern and settings stay on your device. Read the privacy policy.
